4. Data quality and cleaning

Load data and scan to review possible issues.

Automatic checks flag blank cells, duplicate full rows, repeated IDs, and non-numeric values in columns inferred as numeric. A repeated ID is a conflict to investigate, not proof that a row is wrong. No values are silently guessed or corrected.

Manual cell editing

Double-click a cell in the records table, edit its value, then press Enter or click elsewhere. Each edit is added to the change log.

5. Descriptive statistics and charts

Distribution (histogram)

Boxplot and 1.5 × IQR fences

Choose a numeric variable to calculate its distribution and outliers.

Quartiles use the inclusive method: linear interpolation at position (n−1) × p. The 1.5 × IQR fences are calculated once from the full selected dataset and then held fixed for both comparisons.

6. Export cleaned data and change log

Exports use the current working data. The cleaned CSV contains unique rows after any duplicate removal you chose. The change log records edits and cleaning actions, including original/new values, method, and reason. Your original file is not overwritten.
