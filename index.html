02 · My Nepal

A connection to home, culture, and heritage.

Nepal is known for its natural landscapes, cultural diversity, historic heritage, and traditions that continue to shape everyday life.

Natural beauty

Nepal includes the Himalayan region, hills, valleys, rivers, forests, and Mount Everest, the world's highest mountain above sea level.

Culture & festivals
