Data Detectives — CSV Analysis Workbench

Upload · inspect · clean · analyse · export — a reusable tool for your assignments and future datasets

1. Upload CSV file(s) — T1

Choose a CSV file. Files are read locally in this browser; they are not uploaded to a server.

Multiple CSVs are kept as separate datasets. They are not merged, because columns and meanings may differ. Your original data stays separate from the working copy.

2. Dataset summary and suggested data types — T1

3. Search, sort and filter records — T2

Select a column heading to sort ascending or descending. Descriptions and notes are displayed as complete cell text; use horizontal scrolling for wide datasets. Double-click any cell to edit it.

4. Data quality checks and cleaning — T3

Run the scan to inspect missing, invalid, duplicate and conflicting-ID values.

Automatic cleaning rules — preview before applying

Cleaning preview

No correction is assumed to be true just because a value is unusual. Repeated IDs may be legitimate; investigate them before editing. All automated rules show a preview and require your approval. Manual edits are recorded as well.

5. Descriptive statistics and charts — T4 and T5

Histogram

Boxplot and 1.5 × IQR fences

Choose a numeric column and calculate statistics.

Compare with and without outliers

Observations outside the fences

Quartiles use the inclusive percentile method (linear interpolation at position (n − 1) × p). Outlier fences are calculated once from all valid numeric values. The with-outliers-removed comparison uses those same original fences; it does not recalculate them.

6. Export cleaned CSV and change log — T6

The cleaned CSV export removes exact duplicate full rows so the exported records are unique. Repeated IDs with different row contents are not automatically deleted. The change log records cleaning actions and manual edits, including original/new values, method and reason. The original file is never overwritten.
